FAQ

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Who does Give-Directly aim to help?

Our focus is families living in deep poverty in rural villages. Specific locations and eligibility criteria are still being finalized.

Can I apply for help right now?

There is no application process on this website at present. If one opens, we will explain who is eligible and how to apply on our Programs page.

Can I donate through this website?

Yes. Choose a donation amount on the home page and select Donate to continue to PayPal for secure checkout. PayPal also provides the recurring donation option for supporters who want to give monthly.

Is Give-Directly part of GiveDirectly?

Give-Directly is presented here as its own organization. This site does not represent GiveDirectly or use its recipient stories, results, or donation systems.

When will impact information be available?

We will publish verified program and financial information after assistance has been delivered and records are ready to share responsibly.
